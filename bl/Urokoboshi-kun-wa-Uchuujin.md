# 鱗星くんは宇宙人

## Characters
- 鱗星(うろこぼし): 鱗(fish scales)＋星(star) | 青(あお): blue
- 土屋(つちや): 土(soil, earth, ground)＋屋(roof, house, shop)

## Prologue
- 単に(たんに): [Adverb] simply
- 宇宙からやってきた
  - やって来る: [Expression] to come around; to arrive at the present
  - It's more poetic to use やって来る than 来る?

## 第一話
- 委員長(いいんちょう): [Noun] class president
- 浮いて「い」た
  - 浮く(うく): [Verb] to float
- キョロキョロ: [Adverb] looking around (nervously ?)
- ていうか: [Expression] how should I put it; I mean
- 前髪が跳ねている
  - 前髪(まえがみ): [Noun] bangs
  - 跳ねる(はねる): [Verb] to spring up; to burst; to pop (disorganized hair)
- 寝癖がついている
  - 寝癖(ねぐせ): [Noun] bed hair; sleep hair
  - Got messy hair from sleeping
- 懐かれている
  - 懐く(なつく): [Verb] to become attached to
  - B-さんがA-さんに懐かれる: B (receiver) becomes attached to A (doer)
- 相変わらず(あいかわらず): [Adverb] as usual; as always
- 転校する(てんこうする): [Verb] to transfer (to another school)
  - て+くる: [Grammar] "It shows a process, state, or trend that started in the past and is continuing or leading up to the present moment."
- 発光する(はっこうする): [Verb] to emit light
